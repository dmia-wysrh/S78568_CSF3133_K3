About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. Originally established as a Fisheries and Marine Science Centre under Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (UPM) in 1979, it has evolved through several developmental stages—including becoming Universiti Kolej Teknologi Malaysia Terengganu (KUTMT)—before officially being named Universiti Malaysia Terengganu in 2007.

UMT specializes primarily in marine science, aquatic resources, and oceanography. The university plays a pivotal role in national research regarding marine biodiversity, coastal management, and sustainable aquaculture, attracting students and researchers dedicated to preserving aquatic ecosystems.
